Algorithms extracted (for porters)\nThe single shared idiom across all modern patches — spring-damped audio envelope:\n```python\nrms = sqrt(sum(audio_in[i]**2 for i in range(0,100,4))/25)  # subsampled RMS\nforce = rms - env; vel += force*0.4; vel *= 0.85; env += vel; env = clamp(env,0,1)\n```\nand the **veil** persist idiom: `veil=Surface(res); veil.fill(bg); veil.set_alpha(a); screen.blit(veil,(0,0))` at frame start. In our platform: replace veil with a feedback-target shader pass (warp + decay + vignette, exactly the phosphor idiom), and env-RMS with our `ctx.audio.rms_left/right` + bands.\n\nConcrete algorithms read in full, with port notes:\n- **Lorenz attractor** (pysey S - Strange Attractor): 3-ODE integrate (sigma/rho/beta), 120-200 substeps/frame into a 4000-pt ring buffer; project + rotate/pitch, color by z + time; head-glow dot. → **our mesh line strip** (`update_mesh` over a reused vertex table, drawn as fading halo+core like phosphor) with our bands driving sigma/rho; beat-trigger spikes rho. V3D-native.\n- **Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion** (T - Reaction Diffusion): 64x36 grid, Laplacian + `da*la - a*b*b + f*(1-a)` / `db*lb + a*b*b - (k+f)*b`, double-buffered, 1-3 steps/frame, spring-damped feed/`kill` rates modded by audio, trigger reseeds + echo snapshots. → **ideal half-res fragment shader** (640x360 upscaled): per-pixel Gray-Scott in the fragment over a feedback target with a max()/decay composite — nobody has done RD as a GPU shader anywhere in this ecosystem. (Our two encoded traps apply: never bloom inside the sampled target; keep the denominator clamp safe.)\n- **Barnsley Fern IFS** (T - Barnsley Fern): 4 affine fns probabilities p1..p4, 500-3000 pts/frame, audio widens branching (f2_b/f2_c), stereo L/R tilts splay, curl-modulated coefficients, feedback-zoom persistence, 1-3 origins. → **pure Lua points → `mesh` (line/polyline or small circles)**. Cheap on A53; a shader IFS is also feasible but the trail-in-mesh is the classic look.\n- **Whitney Fans** (S - Whitney Fans): two coupled phases p1,p2 (S1=0.017,S2=0.0224), anchor `a` orbits on coupled Lissajous paths (orbit_x/y, drift components), fans of N=10-50 lines at `direction ± u*spread`, center-bright falloff, connecting line. → **trivial `line()`/mesh fan port**; the never-repeating harmonic drift is the payoff. Audio via spring env on spread/speed.\n- **Chladni cymbal contours** (S - Chladni Resonance): f(x,y)=cos(nπx/L)cos(mπy/L)−cos(mπx/L)cos(nπy/L); scan for zero-crossings at step 16 over 720x720, tile 3x3, rotate about center; (n,m) drift + trigger snaps to dramatic pairs. → **perfect half-res shader** (sign of f per pixel → contour glow), which the CPU version never was; or a Lua line-strip of the contour nodes.\n- **Flow-field particles** (T - Flow Field Drift): 600 particles, 22x13 layered-sine angle field (precomputed 360-entry sin/cos tables), steady-rotation, edge respawn, dist-based radius, no veil (particles paint canvas). → **Lua particles → circles or a point-mesh**; 600 circles/frame is heavy at 720p — half-res target or cap ~250.\n- **Cymbal Cymatics** (S - Cymbal Cymatics): circular-plate Bessel nodal patterns; **Stereo Interference** (spatial L/R channel relationship as moiré rings — maps to our true stereo FFT); **Dual Attractor Garden** (two Clifford attractors, botanical); **Lissajous Weaver** (progressive phase offsets — direct ancestor of our phosphor synthetic-Lissajous habit); **Living Voronoi** (drifting seed points + stained-glass tessellation — CPU tessellation, port as mesh or skip); **SMPTE/Words/Branded Backdrop** (label/test/performance text — our `text()` covers).\n- **OSv3 official set** (all immediate-prim): RectGridSOUND (audio z-grid), Boids (flock+obstacle), Folia Angles (63 rotating glyph grid + veil), Woven Feedback (rotation grid + feedback blit — **the stock closest to our feedback-target idiom**), Googly Eyes (LFO-triangle eyes), Spinning Discs (index-palette bitmap — image-dependent, lower priority for a procedural library), Circle/Radial/Perspective scopes (mesh polyline + 3D camera).\n\n---\n\n## 5. Gaps our library should fill (nobody covers these)\n1. **GPU reaction-diffusion / Chladni / Cymbal / attractor fragments** — every existing implementation renders these on CPU (Python grid or point loops). Escaping to a half-res ES2 fragment + upscale is the single biggest library differentiator. Ship `r_d`, `chladni`, `cymatics` scenes.\n2. **True stereo as a first-class axis** — stock OSv3.1 has `audio_in_r` but most modes collapse to mono; our genuine L/R FFT enables stereoscope (XY Lissajous from phosphor), stereo-interference moiré, and channel-difference scenes nobody ships.\n3. **Beat/MIDI-clock-aware scenes** — almost no official mode responds to MIDI clocks (only `MIDI Grid`/`MIDI Note Printer`); our `ctx.midi{clocks,playing,events}` enables quarter-note pulse scenes, transport-synced drops, and clock-quantized geometry — an open niche.\n4. **Multi-scene / macro modes** — ordinaire-x's Cubes (4 scenes in one mode, knob-scene-transition) is the only real precedent; a whole mode category of meta-scenes (one mode, many looks, trigger/knob transitions with wipes) is unfilled.\n5. **Procedural feedback without growing-white** — the veil/feedback techniques all fight self-amplification; our kalibloom `max()`-composite and phosphor decay-warp idioms are the correct answers and should be packaged as several named 'feedback-safe persistence' scenes.\n6. **Mesh-based 3D camera scenes** — stock is 2D; our `begin_camera`/`depth` + `update_mesh` unlocks 3D attractors, perspective grids, and depth-layered particle fields (Perspective Lines, Circle Scope) that stock cannot do.
